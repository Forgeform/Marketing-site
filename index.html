The problem

The owner becomes the system.

We surveyed how small factories actually run: the answer is usually "I'm the only one who knows where everything is." That works — until it doesn't.

No single source of truth

Orders live in a diary, stock in a spreadsheet, dispatches on WhatsApp. Nobody agrees on the number until the owner checks all three.

Cash tied up, invisible

"Profitable on paper" but the money is sitting in WIP and overdue invoices nobody is tracking.

Delivery reliability lost

One missed promise becomes a phone call, then a chasing call, then a client who quietly moves to someone else.

Follow-up is harder than selling

Sales closes the deal, then spends weeks reconstructing status from three systems and a notebook.

Admin eats the day

Certificates, renewals, approvals, reconciliations — all reactive, all last-minute, all on the owner's plate.

No idea how they compare

Is "3 orders at risk" good or bad? There's no benchmark, so every dip feels like a crisis.

How it works

From order to cash, in one flow.

Every manufacturing business runs the same fundamental loop. Factory OS makes each step visible and connected — so a delay in one shows up everywhere it matters.

  1. 1

    Customer places an order

    Sales order raised with line items, quantities and a promised delivery date. The system checks stock and tells you immediately if the date is feasible.

  2. 2

    Planning runs MRP

    Net shortage computed across every open order. Suggested purchase orders generated — you review and approve, the system never buys on its own.

  3. 3

    Goods are received & inspected

    GRN with batch numbers. QC hold if needed. Stock is available only when it passes — the ledger enforces this, nobody can override it.

  4. 4

    Production runs on the floor

    Operators start, pause, complete and record scrap from their phone. Material issues are batch-tracked. The office sees progress in real time.

  5. 5

    Goods are dispatched

    Dispatch recorded against the order. The promised-vs-actual date is captured automatically — no one has to remember to update it.

  6. 6

    Invoice is raised and sent

    GST-compliant tax invoice generated from the dispatch. Print-ready PDF, correct HSN/SAC, correct tax split. Done in one click.

Built for every role

Each person sees exactly what they need.

Not a shared login with a wall of buttons everyone ignores. Factory OS has nine roles, each with its own view and its own permissions.

Owner

Sees everything: revenue, margin, stock value, on-time delivery, exceptions. The morning dashboard answers "what needs me today?"

Plant manager

Production status, capacity, quality holds, dispatch readiness. Knows what's stuck before it becomes a late delivery.

Planner

MRP run, shortages, purchase suggestions, feasibility checks. Plans tomorrow's production from today's real data.

Purchase manager

PO creation, supplier lead times, delivery variance, compliance renewals. Buys what's needed, when it's needed.

Stores

Receipts, issues, transfers, cycle counts, batch tracking. The inventory ledger is append-only — no silent corrections.

Quality

Operator

A phone-first shop floor view: start, pause, complete, record scrap. No training manual required.

Sales

Order status, delivery promises, customer history. Answers "where is my order?" without calling the factory.

Finance

Invoices, receivables ageing, cost of goods, Tally export. Knows what you actually earned.
